The story

Ten years behind a bar taught us what a shirt has to survive.

Bleach on the cuffs. A pen that walks off every night. Pockets that flap into the ice well. A jacket that has to look right at the bus stop at 4 a.m. and not smell like the fryer by Tuesday. We built five pieces around those nights, then shot them in the only light we ever saw them in.
